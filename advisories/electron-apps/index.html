Electron apps

Apps such as VS Code, Cursor, Slack, Postman and Claude carry their own copy of Chromium through Electron, which backports Chrome's security fixes only to its three newest major versions (43, 44 and 45 since 21 Oct 2026). An app built on an older Electron carries every Chrome bug fixed since, including those exploited in the wild such as CVE-2026-125350.

Still patched
Electron 43 to 45; an app on an older Electron is flagged
